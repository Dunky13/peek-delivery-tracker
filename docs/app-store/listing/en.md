## Description

```text
Where’s my parcel? Paste what you have, and Peek finds out.

A tracking number, a carrier link or a whole shipping email: Peek picks out the number and works out which of more than 500 carriers has the parcel. You can also type the number, scan the barcode on the label, or share it to Peek from another app.

Each parcel gets a page of its own: the journey on a map, every scan, and the day it should arrive when the carrier gives one.

No account needed
Parcels you follow without an account are kept on this iPhone, and Peek forgets each one 30 days after it arrives. Sign in for notifications, and to keep your parcels together on your iPhone and on the web.

Will I know when it moves?
With an account, Peek notifies you: every scan, the important steps or delivery day only. Mute a parcel with one tap. Peek can also email you when a parcel is ready for pickup or delivered, only if you turn it on.

Following more than one?
They share one list, refreshed in the background. Search it, filter it, archive what has arrived. A Home Screen widget shows your next delivery and the parcels out for delivery.

Someone else waiting for it?
Share the parcel’s link. Whoever opens it sees how it’s getting on, with most of the tracking number hidden unless you choose to show it. Wrap it as a gift, and what’s inside and who sent it stay a surprise until it’s delivered.

A passport of deliveries
Each delivery stamps your passport: twelve stamps to collect, your delivery times and the countries your parcels were first scanned in. With an account, invite friends with a single-use link and compare passports. They see your delivered count, average delivery time and stamps, never your parcels.

Private and open
• No ads, and Peek sells no data.
• Peek draws its own maps, so no map service sees where your parcels travel.
• Sign in with Apple, Google or a code sent to your email. No password to remember.
• Download your data or delete your account from the app, whenever you like.
• Open source: every line is on GitHub under Apache 2.0. Read it, add a carrier or run your own Peek.

Works with Royal Mail, Evri, DPD, UPS, FedEx, DHL, USPS, Canada Post, Australia Post, Cainiao and hundreds more. Peek is independent: it isn’t affiliated with any carrier, and shows what their own tracking publishes.

Peek speaks English, German, French, Italian, Spanish, Portuguese and Polish, and comes in light and dark.
```
